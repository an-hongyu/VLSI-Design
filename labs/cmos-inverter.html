The propagation delay is B − A; it differs from the 4A/4B measured delay because the input edge here is ideal, not finite-slew.

6. What should students notice?

A balanced beta ratio places the switching point near VDD/2. The output inverts the input.

Model equations

β = μCox(W / L)
βp/βn = (μpCoxWp)/(μnCoxWn)
In(Vin,Vout) = Ip(Vin,Vout)

Equal channel lengths are assumed for the ratio. DC current balance determines the ideal open-circuit output voltage.
