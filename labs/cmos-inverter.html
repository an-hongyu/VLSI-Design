Three worked cases: equations and results

Common design equations βp/βn = [μpCox(Wp/L)] / [μnCox(Wn/L)] VM = [VTN + √r (VDD − |VTP|)] / (1 + √r), where r = βp/βn NML = VIL − VOL · NMH = VOH − VIH
Ask the class: If Wp becomes larger while Wn stays fixed, which way does VM move? Which noise margin shrinks? Which propagation delay increases?
